NEXUS CAM STUDIO 1.1 — PHONE USB MICROPHONE UPDATE

WHY:
The previous phone page sent VIDEO ONLY. This update actually sends and
records the microphone exposed by your phone's browser, separately from
your PC mic. It also fixes packet-jitter/backlog timing that could cut audio.

UPDATE SAFELY:
1. Close the old Nexus app completely.
2. Extract NexusCamStudio-Windows-v1.1.zip into a NEW folder.
3. Launch StartNexusCamStudio.cmd (or dist\NexusCamStudio.exe).
   Check the app window title says 1.1.
Existing settings, history, output folder and local CA stay in your Windows
user profile. This package does not delete or overwrite your recordings.

USB-C MIC SETUP:
1. Connect the USB mic/receiver to the Android phone before opening Chrome.
2. On the PC, Connect Phone and scan the NEW secure QR. Use the same Wi-Fi.
3. Allow camera AND microphone access. On the phone page, tap
   Allow / Refresh Microphones and select the real USB input if listed.
4. Keep Send microphone checked. Use USB / Raw and Mono voice first.
5. Tap START CAMERA. Speak into the external mic. Both real mic meters
   (phone and desktop Phone Microphone) should move.
6. On the PC, Use Phone Mic Only keeps PC microphone OFF (no doubled voice).
   System Audio is unchanged; turn it off too if you want only phone sound.
7. Record and play a 10–15 second clip before recording an important session.

IF ONLY DEFAULT IS LISTED:
Android/Chrome may expose only its Default input. Android controls USB
routing; Nexus cannot invent a separate USB choice. Confirm the external
mic is really being used. Check OTG/cable/power and Android compatibility.

IF AUDIO IS STILL DISTORTED:
Use USB / Raw. If a red clipping warning appears, reduce gain on the USB
microphone/receiver HARDWARE or move it farther away. The PC volume slider
cannot restore already-clipped sound. Keep the phone awake, use stable Wi-Fi,
and avoid recording the same voice from multiple sources.

AUDIO-ONLY PHONE:
Uncheck Send camera on the phone; keep Send microphone on. Record your PC
screen, or select your PC camera again. Switching the phone's camera does
not restart its microphone; changing the actual mic replaces audio only.

VERIFICATION:
Software fixtures tested real WebRTC audio reception and actual saved AAC
sound, including pause/resume and delayed-packet retention. No physical
Android USB mic was available in this environment. Read TEST_REPORT.md.
